Ask for prayer online, anonymously

Some burdens are too heavy to carry alone, and too personal to tell someone face to face. On the Sanctuary prayer wall you can ask for prayer without giving your name. Others pray for you, and you see how many have prayed.

🙏 Post a prayer request

How the prayer wall works

  1. Open the prayer wall in Sanctuary.
  2. Write your request in a sentence or two — no names, phone numbers or addresses.
  3. After a short human review it appears on the wall.
  4. People tap “Pray for this”; the count shows how many have prayed with you.

What people ask prayer for

  • Healing for a parent, a child, a friend
  • Strength during unemployment or money worries
  • Peace in a marriage or a family conflict
  • Guidance before an important decision
  • Thanksgiving for a prayer answered

Kept safe and respectful

Requests are anonymous and reviewed before they appear. Posts with contact details, advertising or anything hurtful are not published, and requests reported by several people are hidden automatically.

A prayer for someone who asked

Lord, you know the person behind this request better than I do. Hold them in what they could not put into words. Give them what they need today, and let them know they are not alone. Amen.
