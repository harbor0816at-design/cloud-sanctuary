Light a candle online for someone

When you cannot be beside someone, you can still pray for them. In Sanctuary, a quiet online church, you walk to the candle chapel, light a candle and entrust that person to God.

🕯 Light a candle now

Who people light candles for

  • A friend or relative who is sick or waiting for surgery
  • Someone who has died, on an anniversary or All Souls’ Day
  • A child facing exams, a new job, a hard decision
  • A thanksgiving: a birth, a healing, an answered prayer
  • Peace in a place at war, or for people you will never meet

How it works

  1. Enter the church — no download, it opens in your browser.
  2. Walk to the candle chapel and choose “Light a candle”.
  3. Write a name or intention. It helps you focus and is never shown publicly.
  4. Stay a moment in the candle’s light and say one sentence to God for them.

A short prayer while your candle burns

Lord, I place this light before you for the one I carry in my heart. Where I cannot reach, may your hand reach. Give rest, healing and peace, and let this small flame keep praying when I fall silent. Amen.

Entering the church, praying and the prayer wall are free. A candle costs €1; when a friend joins through your invitation link, you both receive a free candle.
