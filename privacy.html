隐私与使用说明

云上圣所 · Sanctuary · 更新于 2026 年 11 月

这是什么

云上圣所是一座线上的虚拟教堂，供人随时进入、安静祷告。它采用跨教派适用的祷告、读经与个人省察方式。这里没有虚构的圣职人员。

忏悔室与省察室不是圣事

忏悔室提供的是私密的、引导式的个人认罪与悔改祷告；省察室是免费的简短版本。二者都不是天主教的告解圣事或任何教会的圣礼，也不宣告赦罪。付费购买的是引导内容与体验，不是赦免。如有需要，请联系你所在教会的牧者或神父。

账号

浏览、祷告、点击代祷不需要账号。点蜡烛、进入忏悔室、发起或加入祷告会需要注册账号。我们保存：邮箱、显示名称、经过不可逆加密（bcrypt）处理的密码，以及祷告时长、蜡烛数、忏悔次数、参加集体祷告次数等统计数字。你可以选择是否在祷告会和圣所中显示你的名字。

哪些内容不会离开你的设备

  • 忏悔室和省察室里写下的文字：只存在于当前页面的内存中，不上传、不保存，结束时一键销毁。
  • 「开口说」时的声音：只在本机计算音量，不录音、不上传。
  • 点蜡烛时填写的名字：不上传。
  • 我们只记录「完成了一次忏悔」这件事，不记录任何内容。

哪些内容会发送到服务器

  • 你在圣所里的位置与姿态：实时发给同时在线的访客，用来显示你的身影（如你选择显示名字，也会附上显示名称）。不保存。
  • 代祷墙内容：经人工审核后匿名公开。请勿写真实姓名、电话、地址等信息。
  • 祷告会主题：你发起的祷告会主题和你的显示名称会公开展示，直到祷告会结束。
  • 订单：蜡烛（1 欧元 / 1 美元）和忏悔（9.90 欧元 / 9.90 美元）的订单记录。付款将通过 PayPal 完成，我们不会接触或保存你的银行卡、PayPal 账号密码。
  • 为防止滥用，服务器会保存由网络地址生成的不可逆哈希值，不保存原始 IP 地址。

付款与退款

在线支付开放前为免费测试。开放后，蜡烛和忏悔为一次性的数字体验，开始使用后即视为已提供；若扣款后功能无法使用，请写信给我们，核实后全额退款。

代祷墙与祷告会规则

请彼此尊重。广告、联系方式、攻击他人或任何宗教、群体，以及违法或不当内容，一律不会公开。被多位访客举报的内容会自动隐藏。

危机求助

如果你正处在危机中，或有伤害自己的念头，请立即联系当地的紧急服务或心理援助热线，也可以告诉一位你信任的人。

Privacy & Terms

Sanctuary · a church in the cloud · Updated November 2026

What this is

Sanctuary is a virtual church online, open at any time for quiet prayer. It uses forms of prayer, scripture reading and self-examination shared across Christian traditions. There are no fictional clergy here.

The confessional is not a sacrament

The confessional offers private, guided personal confession and prayers of repentance; the reflection room is a free short version. Neither is the Sacrament of Reconciliation or any church sacrament, and neither pronounces absolution. What you pay for is the guided content and experience, not forgiveness. If you need a sacrament, please contact a pastor or priest of your church.

Accounts

You can browse, pray and pray for others without an account. Lighting candles, the confessional, and starting or joining gatherings require an account. We store your email, display name, a one-way (bcrypt) hash of your password, and simple counts (minutes prayed, candles, confessions, group prayers). You choose whether your name is shown in gatherings and in the sanctuary.

What never leaves your device

  • Anything you write in the confessional or reflection room — it lives only in the page’s memory, is never uploaded or saved, and is erased at the end.
  • Your voice when you “speak” a confession — only the volume is measured on your device; nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Names you type when lighting a candle.
  • We only record that a confession was completed — never its content.

What is sent to our servers

  • Your position and posture in the sanctuary, shared live with other visitors to show your presence (with your display name if you allow it). Not stored.
  • Prayer wall posts, published anonymously after human review. Do not include names, phone numbers or addresses.
  • Gathering topics you create are shown publicly with your display name until the gathering ends.
  • Orders for candles (€1 / $1) and confessions (€9.90 / $9.90). Payments will be completed through PayPal; we never see or store your card or PayPal credentials.
  • To prevent abuse we store a one-way hash derived from your network address, never the raw IP address.

Payments & refunds

Until online payment opens, everything is free (beta). Afterwards, candles and confessions are one-time digital experiences, considered delivered once started. If you were charged but the feature did not work, write to us and we will refund you in full after checking.

Community rules

Please respect one another. Ads, contact details, attacks on people, faiths or groups, and illegal or inappropriate content will not be published. Content reported by several visitors is hidden automatically.

If you are in crisis

If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line right away, or tell someone you trust.

Scripture

Chinese scripture: Chinese Union Version (1919, public domain). English scripture: World English Bible (public domain). Scenes and artwork are original.

Account deletion & contact
