Christmas Eve 2026: light a candle and pray, wherever you are

Christmas Eve 2026 falls on Thursday 24 December, and Christmas Day on Friday 25 December. If you are travelling, working, unwell or far from family, you can still step into a quiet church tonight: Sanctuary is open all night.

🕯 Light a Christmas candle

This page is for you if

  • You are spending Christmas away from home or on your own
  • This is your first Christmas without someone you love
  • You want to light a candle for family far away
  • You miss the candlelight service and want a quiet moment of prayer

A quiet Christmas Eve in Sanctuary

  1. Enter the church in your browser — nothing to install.
  2. Light a candle in the candle chapel for someone you are thinking of.
  3. Pray the Christmas Eve prayer below, or sit in silence with the breathing timer.
  4. At the hour or half hour, join the silent group prayer with people from many countries.

A Christmas Eve prayer

Lord Jesus, you came to us in the quiet of the night, small and poor. Come into this night too. Be near those who are alone, those who grieve an empty chair, those who work while others celebrate. Light in our homes the peace the angels sang of. Amen.

Sanctuary is a place for personal prayer, not a livestreamed service. Many parishes stream their Christmas Eve services; Sanctuary is a quiet place to pray before or after.

Entering the church, praying and the prayer wall are free. A candle costs €1; when a friend joins through your invitation link, you both receive a free candle.
