Advent 2026: dates and a prayer for each week

Advent 2026 begins on Sunday 29 November and ends on Thursday 24 December, Christmas Eve. Four Sundays, four candles on the wreath — one more each week as Christmas comes closer.

🕯 Light this week’s Advent candle

The Sundays of Advent 2026

29 November 2026

First Sunday of Advent · Hope

God of promise, as the year grows dark, light the first candle of hope in me. Teach me to wait without fear, for you are already on your way to us. Amen.

6 December 2026

Second Sunday of Advent · Peace

Prince of Peace, quiet the noise in my heart and in our world. Make me gentle with those near me, and bring your peace where people suffer. Amen.

13 December 2026

Third Sunday of Advent · Joy

Lord, you come close to us. Let a quiet joy grow in me that does not depend on circumstances, and let it overflow to someone who needs it this week. Amen.

20 December 2026

Fourth Sunday of Advent · Love

God who became small for love of us, make room in me as Mary made room for you. Let my love be practical: a visit, a call, a word of forgiveness. Amen.

Christmas Eve: Thursday 24 December 2026 · Christmas Day: Friday 25 December 2026.
